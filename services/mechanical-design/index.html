What does mechanical design include?

  • Enclosures for 3D printing, CNC machining, sheet metal or injection molding.
  • Mechanisms: latches, hinges and actuated parts, such as the timed-release latch of our equestrian dispenser.
  • Thermal considerations, sealing and ingress-protection requirements.
  • DFM and DFA review of plastic and metal parts; tolerancing and fits.
  • Deliverables: 3D CAD (STEP) and manufacturing drawings.

Which process should my enclosure use?

Prototypes and low volumes usually start in 3D printing; CNC and sheet metal suit mid-complexity parts; injection molding pays off once volume justifies tooling. We choose the process with you, based on volume and budget. See additive manufacturing and manufacturing support.
