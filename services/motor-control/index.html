What does motor control design include?

  • Field-oriented control (FOC) for BLDC and PMSM motors: current, speed and position loops.
  • Drives for industrial induction motors.
  • Power stage: inverter topology, gate drive, current and voltage sensing.
  • Haptic and precise-positioning applications, such as knobs with programmable tactile feedback.
  • Protection: overcurrent, overvoltage, overtemperature and fault handling.
  • Thermal design and layout for power electronics.
  • Optional condition monitoring with edge AI.

What is field-oriented control (FOC)?

FOC controls a motor by regulating, separately, the current that produces torque and the current that produces magnetic flux, in a reference frame that rotates with the rotor. Compared with simple six-step commutation it gives smoother torque, higher efficiency and precise control at low speed, which is what haptic feedback and servo-like applications need.
