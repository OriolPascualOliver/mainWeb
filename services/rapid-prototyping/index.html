What is a functional prototype?

A functional ("works-like") prototype behaves like the final product, with real electronics and firmware, even if it does not yet look or cost like it. A "looks-like" model only shows the appearance.

Can you turn my Arduino, ESP32 or Raspberry Pi proof of concept into a custom PCB?

Yes. We take dev-kit proofs of concept to a custom board with structured firmware and an enclosure, keeping the behaviour you already validated.

What happens after the prototype?

Typical next steps are an MVP or a small production run.
