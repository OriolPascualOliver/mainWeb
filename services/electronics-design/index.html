What does electronics design include?

  • Architecture: MCU or SoC selection, power tree, sensors, interfaces and connectivity.
  • Schematic capture with a design review before layout.
  • PCB layout: stack-up, routing, grounding and return-path planning.
  • Power electronics: switching converters, motor drivers, battery and charging circuits.
  • Bring-up on the first boards, debugging and iteration.
  • Manufacturing package: Gerber, BOM with sourcing notes, assembly drawings, pick-and-place.

What do you deliver?

A complete, reviewed design package that a PCB fabricator and assembler can build directly: manufacturing files, BOM, test notes and documentation of the design decisions. If you choose to own the IP, the source design files are handed over too.

Which standards do you design to?

Layout follows IPC-2221 design practice, and boards are designed for manufacturing (DFM) and test (DFT) from the first schematic. EDA tools can be matched to your team so you can maintain the design afterwards.
