What does industrial automation work include?

  • PLC programming for machines and industrial processes.
  • Custom controllers and I/O boards for machines and test equipment.
  • Motor drives and motion control for induction and BLDC motors (see motor control).
  • Sensor integration and condition monitoring, including edge AI for predictive maintenance.
  • Operator controls and interfaces, such as haptic BLDC knobs.
  • Interfaces to the control systems and protocols you already use.
  • Test fixtures and adapters for production and maintenance.

PLC or custom electronics?

A PLC with off-the-shelf modules is the right choice for many machines, and we program them. Custom electronics pays off when standard modules can't meet your size, cost or performance targets, when you need sensing or control they don't offer, or when the equipment will be built in series. Often the best answer combines both.
