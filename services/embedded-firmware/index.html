What does embedded firmware development include?

  • MCU and RTOS-based firmware architecture.
  • Peripheral and sensor drivers, power management and bootloaders.
  • Wireless: BLE, Wi-Fi, LoRa, GNSS and cellular IoT (LTE-M/NB-IoT).
  • Cloud and app integration for data acquisition and remote control.
  • Production firmware: programming procedures and end-of-line test routines.

Why develop firmware and hardware together?

When the same team designs firmware and electronics, pin assignments, power budgets and timing constraints are settled once, and problems found at bring-up are fixed at the source instead of being worked around.
