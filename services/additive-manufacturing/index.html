Which 3D printing process should I choose?

  • FDM (in-house): enclosures, brackets and jigs in engineering plastics.
  • SLA / resin (in-house): fine detail and cosmetic parts.
  • SLS / MJF (via partner): functional end-use parts and snap-fits.
  • Metal (via partner): brackets and heat sinks.

Can 3D printed parts be used in final products?

Yes, at low volumes and with the right material. At higher volumes, CNC, sheet metal or injection molding usually become cheaper per unit.
