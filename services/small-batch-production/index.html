What is included in a small batch?

  • PCB assembly and enclosure parts.
  • Firmware programming and end-of-line functional test.
  • Test fixtures and programming jigs.
  • Serial numbers and per-unit test logs where required.
  • Labeling and packaging.

When should I move to a contract manufacturer?

When volume and design stability justify tooling and minimum order quantities. We tell you honestly when that point arrives and prepare the handover.
