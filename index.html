About

Opotek is a Barcelona-based engineering studio. Consulting engineer Oriol Pascual leads and engineers every project, working with specialist partners (mechanical design, machining, certification labs) when a project needs them.
We take care of the whole product: electronics, firmware, software and mechanical design, and we make sure it all works together on real hardware, not just on paper. You receive tested, validated files that are ready for production.

What we do best

We build complete embedded hardware systems: schematics, PCB design, enclosure CAD, prototyping, and system integration. You get production-ready deliverables (manufacturing files, test documentation and a sourced BOM) and, if you need them, the physical product too: working prototypes or manufactured units.

  • Concept to working prototype. Complete system design: electronics, firmware, and enclosure integrated from day one for your MVP.
  • Validation builds. EVT builds, bring-up, iteration, and validation.
  • Production support. Test fixtures, programming jigs and DFM/DFT review before the first production run.
  • Embedded systems. MCU/RTOS firmware, drivers, and wireless connectivity for IoT (BLE, Wi-Fi, LoRa, GNSS, LTE-M/NB-IoT).
  • AI and machine learning. On-device inference (edge AI), sensor fusion and signal processing, plus AI models built on your product’s data, such as anomaly detection and classification.
  • Industrial automation. PLC programming, custom controllers, motor drives, sensing and operator controls for machines and industrial processes.
  • Turnkey delivery. Coordinated mechanical, electrical, firmware and software in one integrated product.
