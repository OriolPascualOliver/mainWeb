Who leads Opotek?

Oriol Pascual Oliver is the main consultant. He leads the electronics, embedded and edge-AI work, with a particular focus on motor control and power electronics. He does the engineering himself and brings in specialist partners (mechanical design, machining, certification labs) when a project needs them, so you work directly with the engineer on your product.

How do we work?

  1. Discovery callA short meeting to understand what you want to build; then we define the scope and send a tailored quote.
  2. Design & prototypeElectronics, firmware and mechanics are integrated from day one.
  3. ValidationBring-up, iteration and testing on real hardware.
  4. Production handoverManufacturing files, test notes, BOM support and, when needed, test fixtures.

Terms

NDA by default. You decide who owns the intellectual property: if you want it, design files and source code are fully transferred to you.

Where are we based?

Barcelona, Spain.
